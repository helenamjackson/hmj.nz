I'm originally from New Zealand and now live in Seattle, where I work at Microsoft in forward deployed engineering.

I spend a lot of time thinking about how things work, particularly software, organizations, and the people behind them. I'm interested in how AI is changing the way we build and do things, how knowledge gets shared, and how small teams can do surprisingly ambitious work.
